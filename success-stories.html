Partnership with IDP IELTS

Telangana Minorities Study Circle (TGMSC) is proud to be associated with IDP IELTS as a Referral Partner. Through this partnership, TGMSC supports and guides students and aspirants who wish to pursue the IELTS (International English Language Testing System) examination for their academic, professional, and international opportunities. Our association with IDP IELTS reflects our commitment to providing aspirants with access to reliable IELTS-related guidance and a recognised pathway towards their global aspirations.

Aspirants who overcame economic barriers and secured their dreams through structured preparation.

VR

"I wanted to pursue my Master's abroad but had never taken a language proficiency test in my life. Through the Centre's IDP IELTS referral guidance, I got a structured practice plan for all four bands and walked into the test without any fear. I scored a 7.0 overall."

"Preparing for TSPSC Group-II without coaching seemed impossible because private institutes charged over ₹40,000. Here, top faculty taught us Telangana history, polity and current affairs free of cost. I secured Rank 42 in my community category."
