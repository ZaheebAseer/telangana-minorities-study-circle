Corporate Partnership

Why Partner with the Centre?

The Telangana Minorities Study Circle prepares thousands of ambitious candidates every year with disciplined analytical, problem-solving, and communication training.

🎯

Pre-Screened Talent

Access candidates rigorously tested in quantitative aptitude, reasoning, data interpretation, and English proficiency.

🏛️

Zero Recruitment Cost

As a government public service initiative, all recruitment facilitation, campus drives, and venue allocations are 100% free of charge.

🌐

Statewide Reach

Connect with high-potential candidates across all 33 districts of Telangana, promoting diversity and regional inclusion.

🏢

Modern Drive Venues

Leverage our smart classrooms, air-conditioned computer labs, and auditorium at Nampally, Hyderabad for your recruitment drives.

Candidate Pool

Diverse Educational Backgrounds

Engineering & IT

B.Tech / B.E. graduates in CS, IT, ECE with problem-solving and software foundational training.

Commerce & Finance

B.Com, M.Com, and BBA graduates trained in banking operations, accounting, and quantitative aptitude.

Sciences & Arts

Graduates in life sciences, mathematics, economics, and humanities with strong verbal and analytical skills.

Collaboration Tracks

Ways to Engage with the Centre

  • → Campus Recruitment Drives: Conduct dedicated offline or virtual interview drives for full-time job openings.
  • → Internships & Apprenticeships: Offer 3 to 6-month hands-on internships to final-year and recent graduates.
  • → Job & Career Fairs: Set up company stalls and interview booths during state and district job melas.
  • → Industry Guest Lectures: Deliver masterclasses on corporate expectations, emerging technologies, and career growth.

Employer Registration

Register your organisation to post job/internship openings and schedule campus recruitment drives.
