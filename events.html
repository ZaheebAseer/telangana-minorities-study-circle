Event Highlights

Independence Day Celebrations 2026

On 15th August 2026, the Centre celebrated the 79th Independence Day with flag hoisting, an NCC guard of honour, and an address to students and staff at the Nampally campus.

Flag Hoisting Ceremony

The Director hoists the National Flag at the campus entrance, joined by staff and NCC cadets.

NCC Guard of Honour

The NCC cadets' contingent presents the tricolour after the flag hoisting ceremony.

Director Addressing the Cadets

Director Syed Tariq Anwar interacts with the NCC cadets who took part in the ceremony.

Special Address to Staff & Students

A visiting senior official addresses staff and students on the significance of the day.

Interactive Classroom Session

Aspirants interact with visiting officials during a classroom session held as part of the day's programme.

Faculty & Staff Group Photo

Faculty, mentors, and administrative staff come together to mark the occasion.

Independence Day 2026 — Full Assembly

Staff, faculty, NCC cadets and students gather for a commemorative photograph after the celebrations.

Classroom Orientation Session

An interactive orientation and mentorship session conducted for aspirants at the Centre.

IELTS Awareness Outreach

The team collaborates on an IELTS awareness drive to guide students on international study and work pathways.
