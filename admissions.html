Admissions & Intake

How to Join the Centre — Free Coaching Admissions

100% government-sponsored coaching, digital library access, and career guidance for notified minority community youth.

Who Can Apply

Admission Eligibility Criteria

Candidates fulfilling the following guidelines are eligible to enrol in our free coaching programmes.

👥

Minority Status

Must belong to notified minority communities: Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Parsi, or Jain.

📍

Telangana Domicile

Must be a resident/domicile of Telangana with a valid residential or community certificate.

🎓

Educational Criteria

Graduation (Degree) for UPSC, TSPSC Groups & Banking; Intermediate/10+2 for Constable and SSC CHSL.

📋

Income Guidelines

Annual parental/family income within prescribed Government of Telangana minority welfare thresholds.

Admissions Workflow

Simple 4-Step Admission Process

Follow these steps to secure admission in the upcoming batch.

1
Check Eligibility

Review notification guidelines, educational qualifications, and community eligibility.

2
Submit Application

Fill out the online form or visit your nearest District Minority Welfare Study Centre.

3
Counselling Session

Participate in an aptitude assessment to place you in the optimal coaching stream.

4
Begin Classes

Receive your student ID card, study materials, library access pass, and class timetable.
