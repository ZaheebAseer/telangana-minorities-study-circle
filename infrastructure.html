Campus & Facilities

Modern, Technology-Enabled Campus Infrastructure

Purpose-built facilities designed to support disciplined preparation, research, and collaborative learning under one roof.

Learning Ecosystem

World-Class Learning Amenities

State-of-the-art facilities provided free of cost to empower aspiring minority candidates.

🖥️

Smart Classrooms

Acoustically treated lecture halls equipped with ultra-HD interactive smart boards, audio-visual lecture capture systems, high-speed Wi-Fi, and ergonomic seating for intensive learning sessions.

📚

Digital Library & E-Resource Lab

Over 100 high-speed computer terminals providing authenticated access to 10,000+ digital books, national competitive exam test series, online journals, and searchable previous-year question archives.

📖

Dedicated Study Halls

Air-conditioned silent reading rooms and dedicated individual study carrels open 14 hours daily (08:00 AM to 10:00 PM) to cultivate rigorous self-study and revision routines.

📑

Reference Library

Curated physical collection of 15,000+ reference volumes covering NCERT standards, advanced GS titles, constitutional law, history, current affairs magazines, and daily national dailies in English, Telugu, and Urdu.

🛏️

Residential & Dining Facilities

Well-maintained hostel facilities and hygienic dining arrangements provided for selected full-time residential batches preparing for intensive Civil Services and Group-I examinations.

🏃

Sports & Recreation Spaces

Outdoor physical training tracks, badminton courts, and indoor recreation zones supporting physical endurance conditioning for SI/Police aspirants and mental rejuvenation for all students.
