Career Counselling Centre

From Classroom to Career — Guidance at Every Step

A dedicated Career Guidance Cell linking students to competitive examinations, government employment, private-sector careers and emerging job sectors.

Dr. Sara Shuttari

Director, Career Counselling & Student Mentorship

"Our core mission is to empower every young minority aspirant with objective aptitude assessment, strategic career roadmaps, and the practical confidence required to succeed in competitive examinations and contemporary industry roles."

🧭

Career Guidance & Planning

Personalized one-on-one sessions helping school, intermediate, and college students navigate decisive academic junctures.

  • → Career Awareness: Orientation to central, state, banking, defence, and corporate career paths.
  • → Course Selection: Objective guidance on diploma, degree, and professional certifications.
  • → Career Planning: Multi-year roadmaps balancing degree education with competitive exams.
  • → Aptitude Assessment: Scientific evaluation of quantitative, verbal, and analytical strengths.
💼

Private-Sector Employability

Bridging the gap between academic qualifications and industry hiring standards through direct employer collaboration.

  • → Job & Career Fairs: Annual and district-level placement drives with leading corporations.
  • → Employer Interaction: Direct interaction with HR leaders, tech managers, and alumni mentors.
  • → Internships & Apprenticeships: Practical industry exposure for final-year students.
  • → Resume & Interview Prep: Professional CV writing, mock interviews, and group discussions.
Student Development

Holistic Development Beyond Textbooks

The Centre believes that success in competitive examinations and professional careers requires more than rote academic knowledge. Therefore, structured emphasis is placed on the holistic personal and mental development of every student.

Regular interactive workshops, group discussion forums, and confidence-building exercises help aspirants overcome hesitation, refine their thought process, and present themselves with authority.

Key Development Areas:

  • → Analytical Reasoning
  • → Communication Skills
  • → English Proficiency
  • → General Awareness
  • → Current Affairs Analysis
  • → Digital Literacy
  • → Leadership Qualities
  • → Confidence Building
  • → Time Management
  • → Stress Management
  • → Personality Grooming
  • → Ethical Governance
Academic Support & Assessment

Our 8-Step Structured Preparation System

A proven, disciplined framework ensuring thorough coverage, continuous assessment, and interview readiness.

1
Orientation

Syllabus mapping, strategy, and booklist guidance.

2
Foundation Classes

Core conceptual clarity across all subjects.

3
Subject Preparation

In-depth lecture modules and note-making.

4
Practice Tests

Weekly topic-wise objective & descriptive tests.

5
Mock Examinations

Full-length simulations under real exam conditions.

6
Performance Analysis

One-on-one review of strengths & weak areas.

7
Revision

Structured high-yield revision and doubt clearing.

8
Interview Guidance

Mock interview boards with senior civil servants.

Continuous evaluation ensures candidates track measurable progress toward qualifying standards.
