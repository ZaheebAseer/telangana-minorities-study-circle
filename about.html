Institutional Overview

Who We Are & Our Mandate

A dedicated Government of Telangana initiative under the Minorities Welfare Department providing structured guidance and free coaching to minority youth.

Who We Are

A dedicated space for minority students to prepare, question and grow.

The Telangana Minorities Study Circle & Career Counselling Centre is dedicated to providing quality coaching, academic guidance and comprehensive support to minority students and aspirants preparing for Central and State Government competitive examinations.

The Centre provides a structured, disciplined learning environment supported by experienced in-house faculty, specialised guest faculty, modern teaching technology, digital learning resources and comprehensive reference materials — enabling talented and deserving minority students to compete confidently and pursue rewarding careers in Government, Public Sector, Banking, Police, Administration and other employment sectors.

What guides our work

  • → Systematic coaching for UPSC, TSPSC, SSC, SI, Police and other Central & State Government examinations
  • → Regular tests, assessments and performance reviews to track progress
  • → Current-affairs training and personality & interview-skills development
  • → Career counselling based on each student's qualifications and interests
  • → Encouraging students from economically and socially disadvantaged backgrounds to pursue competitive careers

Our Vision

To emerge as a leading centre for competitive examination coaching, career guidance and skill development for minority youth — enabling access to quality education, employment opportunities and meaningful participation in public services.

Our Mission

To identify and nurture talent among minority students through expert academic guidance, examination-oriented training, mentoring and technology-enabled learning — while building the analytical, communication and problem-solving abilities students need to succeed.

Objectives

Our Core Institutional Objectives

Systematic, transparent, and student-focused goals guiding all departmental activities.

01

Equal Opportunity

Ensure that deserving youth from minority communities have barrier-free access to high-calibre preparation for elite state and central civil service examinations.

02

Structured Mentorship

Provide one-on-one academic counselling, answer-writing feedback, and syllabus breakdown through experienced civil servants and domain experts.

03

District Reach

Extend counselling, study materials, and mock exam facilities across all 33 districts of Telangana through decentralized minority welfare centers.

04

Digital Inclusion

Bridge the digital divide by equipping aspirants with high-speed internet, e-library collections, and computer-based examination simulators.

05

Employability Readiness

Prepare graduates for banking, corporate, and private-sector job opportunities through certified personality development and aptitude training.

06

Holistic Support

Provide library access, study halls, nutrition support, and residential facilities for intensive coaching batches to ensure uninterrupted study.

Our Goal

"Quality Education. Competitive Excellence. Equal Opportunity. Empowered Youth."

The Telangana Minorities Study Centre strives to become a platform through which minority students can develop the knowledge, skills, confidence and determination necessary to succeed in competitive examinations and build successful careers in Government, Public Sector, Banking, Police, Administration, IT and other emerging employment sectors.

Student-Centred Learning Environment

The Centre creates an environment where students can learn, study, practise, interact with experts and prepare for examinations under one roof.

The combination of:
Experienced Faculty + Smart Classrooms + Digital Library + Study Halls + Reference Materials + Technology Support + Residential/Dining Facilities + Sports & Recreation

provides students with an integrated ecosystem for competitive-examination preparation.

Commitment to Minority Youth

The Telangana Minorities Study Centre is committed to creating opportunities for talented and aspiring minority youth by providing quality coaching, academic resources, career guidance and a supportive learning environment.

The Centre's broader objective is to help students move from education to employability, competitive preparation to achievement, and aspiration to meaningful career opportunities.
